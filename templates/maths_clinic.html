Two Editions and Counting

Maths Clinic 1.0 (2024) & 2.0 (2025)

Recognising that academic confidence is part of a student's overall sense of purpose, OFMA launched the Maths Clinic to give students in Okeho free, structured support in mathematics — one of the subjects students most often struggle with, and one that shapes access to further education and careers.

The first edition, Maths Clinic 1.0, ran from 5th to 23rd August 2024, specially designed for girls transitioning from SS2 to SS3. The clinic has since continued to grow alongside OFMA's other programmes.
