What We Cover

From JAMB to graduation day

For our first two scholarship students, OFMA is covering the full cost of getting — and staying — in university:

  • JAMB registration
  • Post-UTME coaching & registration
  • Accommodation throughout their studies
  • Full tuition fees, year on year, until graduation

This isn't a one-time gift — it's a multi-year commitment to two young women's futures, and we're covering it because we believe it's worth doing properly.

Post-UTME Coaching Eligibility

Our scholarship pipeline starts with a sponsored Post-UTME coaching programme, open to:

  • Female Muslim indigenes of Okeho
  • UTME score of 200 and above
  • Seeking admission into a recognised tertiary institution

Benefits: Sponsored Post-UTME class at Extramural, Agbowo, Ibadan, with free accommodation throughout. The overall best-performing candidate receives a full university scholarship.
