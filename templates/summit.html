About This Year

Uncompromised, on purpose.

This year's theme speaks to a challenge many of us feel quietly every day — the pressure to soften our values, dilute our faith, or shrink our ambitions just to fit in. Over two days, the summit brings together scholars, professionals, students, and families for sessions on faith, leadership, parenting, and purposeful living.

Programme

Two days, three major sessions

Day 1 — Saturday, 19th December 2026

Al-Istiqamah — Steadfastness on Allah's Path in Uncertain Times

In an era of shifting moral baselines and digital distraction, this session explores Al-Istiqamah not as passive endurance, but as an active, uncompromised commitment to Allah's commands — building the internal certainty (Yaqeen) to remain anchored in faith on campus, at work, and at home.

Day 2 — Sunday, 20th December 2026

The Ideal Muslim Home

Intentionality, boundaries, and the psychology of family dynamics — addressed from both an Islamic and a psychological perspective, examining role-bleeding, self-neglect, and the protection paradox in parenting.

Day 2 — Sunday, 20th December 2026

Building a Halal Brand

Turning small ideas into lasting wealth — an introduction to Nigeria's growing halal economy and how ethical entrepreneurship becomes a pathway to sustainable wealth creation.

Both Days

Debate & Quiz Competition

Secondary school students compete for prizes, with awards for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place across both the debate and quiz competitions.
