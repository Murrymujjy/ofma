Past Summits

Every summit builds on the one before it. Here's a look back at where OFMA has been.

2025

OFMA Annual Summit 6.0 — "A Journey Called Purpose"

Held 20th–21st December 2025 at Okeho Central Mosque, featuring Imam AbdurRahman Ahmad, Prof. Kareem, Sis Toyyibah Ibraheem, and Mrs. Sideeqoh, alongside a debate competition and panel sessions.

2024

OFMA Annual Summit 5.0 — "Tides & Strides"

Held 21st–22nd December 2024 in Okeho. This edition also saw the launch of the maiden Al-Qawareer Magazine at Okeho Central Mosque on 22nd December 2024.

2023

OFMA Annual Summit 4.0 — "Sisterhood Synergy: Balancing Excellence in Every Aspect"

Held 16th–17th December 2023 at Okeho Central Mosque, Gbonje Market. A two-day programme split by audience — Day 1 for women and teens (parenting, vocational training, modesty, academic focus), Day 2 open to all (Islamic finance, gratitude & tawakkul) — with Alh. Soliu Shittu as Keynote Speaker and Alhaji Sheriffdeen Ahmed as Chairman of the Day.

2022

OFMA Sisters' Summit 3.0 — "Our Purpose: Between Living & Existence"

Held 10th–11th December 2022 at Okeho Central Mosque, Gbonje Market, Okeho, Oyo State — featuring seasoned lecturers, networking, halal fun and games, and personal development sessions.

2021

OFMA Sisters' Summit 2.0 — "Towards Enlivening Our Purpose"

Held 18th–19th December 2021 at Okeho Central Masjid, Gbonje Market, Kajola Local Government, Oyo State. Speakers included Saheedat Tobiloba Adetayo (Executive Director, Pad A Queen Initiative).

2020

OFMA 1.0 — "Within the Circumference: Career, Relationship & Worship"

OFMA's very first public lecture, held 19th December 2020 at Okeho Central Mosque — featuring Dr. Ganiyah Tijani-Adenle, Sis. Ruqayyah Thaabit, Mrs. Lateefah Abifarin, and Sis. Kafeelah Busari.
