Our Story

A small, deliberate investment made every year in the same conviction: that our community's future is only as strong as the guidance its young people receive today.

{% include "partials/_curve_divider.html" %}
2018

An Idea Is Born

OFMA begins as a mere idea, born from a desire to give Muslim women and girls in Okeho a space to grow in faith, confidence, and purpose.

2019

OFMA Is Founded

The Okeho Female Muslims' Ambassadors is formally established, with a mission to empower Muslim women and teens through faith-rooted education and community service.

2020

OFMA 1.0 — First Public Lecture

OFMA holds its first public lecture, themed "Within the Circumference: Career, Relationship & Worship," on 19th December 2020 at Okeho Central Mosque.

2021 – 2024

Sisters' Summit 2.0 through Annual Summit 5.0

The summit grows year on year — through themes of purpose, existence, sisterhood, and resilience — culminating in the launch of OFMA's maiden magazine, Al-Qawareer, in December 2024.

2024

OFMA Maths Clinic 1.0

OFMA launches its first Maths Clinic, extending its work into academic support for students in the community.

2025

Maths Clinic 2.0 & Annual Summit 6.0

The Maths Clinic returns for a second edition. OFMA Annual Summit 6.0, themed "A Journey Called Purpose," brings together scholars, professionals, and students for a multi-day programme of faith and leadership.

2026

OFMA Scholarship Programme Launches

OFMA begins sponsoring the full university journey — JAMB, Post-UTME, accommodation, and tuition — for its first two scholarship students.

2026

OFMA Annual Summit 7.0

Themed "Uncompromised: Standing Firm When the World Demands Less," this year's summit holds December 19–20 at FUNATO, Okeho — our deepest and widest edition yet.

Every edition builds on the last.
